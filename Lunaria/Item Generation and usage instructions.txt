## ITEM INSTRUCTIONS
 
### NEVER Update For
- Items merely seen, discussed, offered, displayed, inspected, or nearby
- Storage or reorganization (putting items in bags, packs, pouches, pockets) -- NEVER a remove
- Other characters' actions on their own inventory
- Trades or transfers between NPCs
- Unconfirmed offers, quotes, negotiations, or stated prices without player agreement
 
### Non-Combat Item Changes
 
ADD only when an item physically enters the player's possession -- taken, picked up, received, looted, handed to them, or crafted by them.
REMOVE only when an item physically leaves the player's possession -- handed over, expended, consumed, discarded, destroyed, or taken from them.
 
Searching or looting a body, container, or location implies taking found items unless the player explicitly states otherwise.
 
Completed transactions are TWO-WAY: if currency is removed for a purchase, the received goods MUST be added. If goods are handed over, payment MUST be added.
 
TRANSACTION TIMING: A transaction requires BOTH a stated price AND the player explicitly paying or agreeing AFTER the price is stated. "I'll take it" before a price is quoted is NOT confirmation. An NPC placing items on a counter is NOT a completed transaction. Only update when the player actually hands over credits or confirms the final price.
 
Quantities must match what actually changed. Do not add or remove an entire stack unless the story clearly shows the full stack changed hands or was consumed.
 
Perform the update immediately once actual change of hands occurs.
 
### Combat Item Rules
 
Consumables (stims, grenades, ammo packs, disposable charges) are removed when used in combat.
 
EQUIPMENT DURABILITY: Normal combat does NOT damage or destroy equipped weapons or armor. Hits, deflections, impacts, and near-misses are expected wear. Vivid narration -- sparks off plating, scorch marks, dents from rounds -- is dramatic flavor, NOT mechanical damage.
 
Equipment damage ONLY occurs from EXTRAORDINARY events:
- A targeted attack explicitly intended to destroy the specific item
- Environmental destruction (plasma immersion, focused beam weapons, EMP overload, reactor breach, decompression)
- Magitech or psionic effects specifically described as shattering or unmaking gear
- The player deliberately sacrificing or abandoning an item
- Explicit narrative statement that the item is broken beyond use
 
If an extraordinary event occurs: remove the original and add a "Damaged [Item Name]" version, equipping it automatically. A "Damaged" item still functions and is only destroyed if ANOTHER extraordinary event specifically targets it. 
 
When uncertain, preserve the item.
 
### Tier Naming
 
When a player acquires a NEW equippable item (weapon, armor, implant, accessory) or consumable items like stims, injectors, or combat drugs ONLY, not already in their inventory or the world item list, ALWAYS prepend a rarity tier.
 
Tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
- Common: surplus shops, station vendors, standard-issue, basic finds
- Uncommon: mission rewards, skilled fabrication, superior quality, NPC gifts
- Rare: advanced tech, prototype systems,restricted-zone salvage, major mission rewards
- Epic: boss drops, faction elite gear, named weapons with history
- Legendary: Trymarch-origin items, objects of galactic significance
 
Format: "[Tier] [Item Name]" -- e.g. "Common Gauss Pistol", "Rare Turbocharged Plasma Rifle".
Flavor items have NO tier prefix -- e.g. "Ration Pack", "Datapad", "Access Card", "Crew Manifest".
 
MATERIAL TIERS: Crafting materials also get tier prefixes.
- Common: ordinary, basic, widely available, bulk salvage
- Uncommon: high-grade, refined, premium, military-surplus
- Rare: exotic compounds, alien-derived, pre-Builder origin
- Epic: from extremely dangerous or unique sources
- Legendary: Trymarch/Einmarch materials, Luxfractian substances
 
Do NOT tier-prefix:
- Items already in inventory or the world item list (reuse exact existing names)
- Non-equippable or flavor items: documents, food, drinks, tools, keycards, misc objects etc
 
### Crafting Accuracy
 
When crafting occurs, remove ONLY the specific materials the story describes being consumed. Match material names exactly from inventory.
 
### Category and Slot Reference
 
Armor: head, body, cloak, legs, feet, hands
Weapon: weapon
Consumable: consumable
Implant (engine category Necklace): necklace
Implant (engine category Ring): ring
Accessory: accessory
Miscellaneous: no slot
Currency: no slot
 
### Before Output, Confirm
1. Did items physically change possession? If not, output nothing.
2. Does any entry match the NEVER list? If yes, remove it.
3. For purchases: did the player confirm AFTER a price was stated?
4. Am I removing equipped gear? Was there an extraordinary destruction event? If just normal combat, preserve the item.
