# OVERRIDE RULES -- THESE TAKE PRIORITY OVER ANY CONFLICTING INSTRUCTIONS ABOVE
 
Where earlier guidance and these rules disagree, follow these rules without exception.
 
## Difficulty Framework (overrides Difficulty Scale)
 
Difficulty reflects character capabilities against the challenge, not story convenience.
- **Easy**: Well within the character's skills. Execution is smooth, success reliable.
- **Medium**: Matches ability level. Fair uncertainty, success depends on timing and technique. Minimum for combat. Minimum for acting while injured, exhausted, poisoned, or under duress.
- **Somewhat Hard**: Capable but facing complications -- unfamiliar terrain, partial info, skilled opposition, time pressure. Mistakes create real setbacks.
- **Hard**: Opposition holds advantage through superior skill, power, or positioning. Demands planning and exploitation of openings.
- **Very Hard**: Severely outmatched or constrained. Success requires exceptional execution or external help. Failure is likely and costly.
- **Extremely Hard**: Exceeds reasonable limits. Survival, retreat, or delay are the only realistic outcomes.
 
Use the highest applicable skill or attribute bonus. Factor in skills, attributes, equipment, injuries, fatigue, resource state, preparation, terrain, visibility, timing, and opposition competence.
 
## Social Difficulty
 
NPC disposition, stakes, and plausibility determine social difficulty:
- Friendly NPC, reasonable request: Simple or Easy.
- Neutral NPC, minor persuasion or routine haggling: Easy or Medium.
- Suspicious or reluctant NPC, contested request: Somewhat Hard.
- Hostile, perceptive, or high-status NPC, implausible request: Hard or higher.
- Deception against someone with reason to distrust: one tier harder than equivalent persuasion.
 
## Travel Override
 
Travel through hazardous, unfamiliar, or hostile environments (vacuum, radiation, debris fields, derelict interiors, zero-g without tethers, hostile station sectors) requires normal difficulty assessment. Only travel through safe, familiar, unobstructed areas qualifies as Simple.
 
## Repeated Actions
 
Repeated attempts at the same failed action in a scene increase difficulty by one tier per retry unless circumstances meaningfully change.
 
## Required Items Override
 
Combat abilities or specialized techniques attempted without their required weapon or tool type: -50 penalty.
 
## Crafting Difficulty
 
Tier determines crafting difficulty:
- Common: Easy. Basic materials and technique.
- Uncommon: Medium. Solid skill and quality materials required.
- Rare: Hard. High proficiency, rare materials, proper tools or workshop. Mistakes waste materials.
- Epic: Very Hard. Master-level skill, exceptional materials and conditions.
- Legendary: Extremely Hard. legendary materials, extraordinary circumstances.
 
Crafting skills by output: armory (weapons/armor/ammunition), engineering (mechanical devices/structural/vehicles), electronics (gadgets/sensors/comms), cybernetics (implants/prosthetics/augments), mech tech (mech parts/ASAC/mech weapons), magitech (magitech items/arcane-tech hybrids), chemistry (pharmaceuticals/explosives/reagents), cooking (food).
 
Crafting without the relevant skill: two tiers harder. Without appropriate tools or workshop: one tier harder. Penalties stack.

On failure: materials consumed, produce flawed or lower-tier result. Critical failure destroys materials.
 
Modification is separate from base crafting. Item must exist before modifying. Same tier scale. Skill matches mod type (armory for weapons/armor, electronics for tech, cybernetics for implants, magitech for arcane augmentation). Failed modification wastes mod components but preserves the base item.
 
## Gathering Difficulty
 
Material tier determines gathering difficulty:
- Common (scrap metal, polymer sheets, salvaged wiring): Easy. Accessible sites, basic effort.
- Uncommon (steel plating, carbon fiber, silicon wafers): Medium. Requires knowing where to look.
- Rare (nanocarbon weave, quantum capacitors, resonance crystals): Hard. Deep-orbit sites, magitech foundries, anomalous zones.
- Epic (antimatter cells, neural lace, Progenitor fragments): Very Hard. Classified installations, astrophysical hazards, buried Progenitor ruins.
- Legendary (Tyrmarch metamaterials, Eirenarch defense cores, Luxfracta exotic alloys): Extremely Hard. Xeno-held territory, sealed alien vaults. Finding the source is often harder than extraction.
 
Gathering skills: engineering (ores/alloys/industrial salvage), survival (wilderness/biological/wreck scavenging), chemistry (reagents/compounds/organic samples), xenotechnology (xeno-materials from alien sites).
 
Without relevant skill: one tier harder. In hostile conditions (combat, hazards, time pressure): one tier harder. Penalties stack.
 
Quantity scales with success margin. Exceptional success yields bonus materials.
