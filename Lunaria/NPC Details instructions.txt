HIGHEST PRIORITY INSTRUCTIONS
TYPE OVERRIDE: If the NPC type entry contains a DETAILS GENERATION OVERRIDE section, follow those instructions instead of the format below.

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS FOR hiddenInfo:
Determine whether the character is SENTIENT (any intelligent, speaking creature) or NOT-SENTIENT (animal, drone, non-intelligent construct or robot).
--- IF SENTIENT NPCs ---
Write hiddenInfo as three labeled fields. Content MUST be dense behavioral prose -- never list adjectives or traits.
Occupation is one fact about the NPC. Background, personality, and abilities must include dimensions unrelated to their job.
Background: 8-10 sentences covering ALL of the following:
1. Name, age, race, faction, occupation, and how they earn their living. Note visible augmentation level or its absence -- this is a social marker.
2. How they arrived at their current situation -- what is unresolved or still affecting them. Not a full biography.
3. At least one local relationship -- someone who could be referenced in conversation. Describe the dynamic, not just the connection.
4. What they currently want or care about. Can be mundane, satisfied, contradictory, or self-interested.
5. One or two opinions about the world around them -- their faction, the war, command decisions, other species. Specific and voiced, not abstract.
Personality: 9-11 sentences covering ALL of the following:
1. How they talk. Sentence length, vocabulary, verbal habits. What makes them talkative versus terse.
2. Default social behavior AND what changes it -- always pair these. Do not write single static traits like "she is cautious."
3. Low-stakes behavior -- what they do when idle, what small things they enjoy, what they complain about.
4. Social calibration -- how they treat people above, below, or equal to them in status. For caste species, how rigidly do they observe hierarchy in private versus public?
5. What they deflect or lie about, if anything. Many characters are simply open or indifferent -- not everyone hides something.
6. One area of real competence or strong conviction beyond their occupation.
Every point above should get roughly equal space.
Combat: 5-6 sentences covering ALL of the following:
1. Weapon preference, engagement range, use of cover and tech -- shields, targeting, neural-linked aim.
2. What makes them start, escalate, or abandon a fight. How they react when wounded or shields drop.
3. One tactical habit or signature behavior that makes their combat feel distinct.
4. Combat-trained characters fight with doctrine. Civilians react consistent with their background. Species physiology matters -- saurians are tougher, beastkin faster, orcs hit harder.
Format: "hiddenInfo": "Background: ...\n\nPersonality: ...\n\nCombat: ..."
--- IF NOT-SENTIENT ---
Write hiddenInfo as two labeled fields. Describe what the creature or machine does, not what it "is."
Behavior: 3-5 sentences. Biological: habitat, territorial patterns, sensory strengths, threat escalation, signs of presence. Drones/constructs: patrol pattern, sensor triggers, operational limits, what causes malfunction.
Combat: 3-5 sentences. How it initiates attacks, preferred range, signature offense, reactions when damaged or outnumbered, what triggers retreat or shutdown. Must feel distinct to fight.
Format: "hiddenInfo": "Behavior: ...\n\nCombat: ..."

ANTI-PATTERNS -- NEVER DO THIS:
Trait lists disguised as prose ("He is brave, cunning, and loyal")
Static or unpaired traits ("She is suspicious") -- write behavioral pairs: who, when, what changes it
Personality that restates the NPC's role ("meticulous logistics officer") -- write behavior beyond the job
Every NPC hiding secret pain behind a cheerful mask -- some people are straightforward
Every soldier questioning the war and suffering trauma -- some are professionals doing a job
Every orc written as a stupid brute -- intelligence is a weapon too
Relationships outside the current location that can never appear in scene
Generic reactions ("gets angry when threatened") -- only include responses specific to THIS character
Personality for drones or combat philosophy for maintenance bots
Repeating information across fields or abilities the character could not plausibly have
Every sentence starting with "He" or "She" -- vary structure


Abilities
ABILITY GENERATION RULES:
Civilian: 4-6 abilities. Combat-trained: 7-10 abilities. Drone/Beast: 3-5 (instinct or programming driven, no social).
Each ability is a broad category, not a single move. Mix of attack, utility, and social for sentient NPCs. At least one attack.
Format: (<TYPE>) <ABILITY NAME>: <ABILITY DESCRIPTION>
Example: (attack) Gauss Rifle Proficiency: Accurate and disciplined with magnetic accelerator weapons at medium-to-long range, using cover transitions and neural-linked targeting to maintain fire on moving targets.
Adapt to each character. Do not copy the example.
