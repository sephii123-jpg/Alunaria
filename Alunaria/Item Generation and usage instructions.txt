## ITEM INSTRUCTIONS

### NEVER Update For
- Items merely seen, discussed, offered, displayed, inspected, or nearby
- Storage or reorganization (putting items in bags, packs, pouches, pockets) -- NEVER a remove
- Other characters' actions on their own inventory
- Trades or transfers between NPCs
- Unconfirmed offers, quotes, negotiations, or stated prices without player agreement

### Non-Combat Item Changes

ADD only when an item physically enters the player's possession -- taken, picked up, received, looted, handed to them, or crafted by them.
REMOVE only when an item physically leaves the player's possession -- handed over, expended, consumed, discarded, destroyed, or taken from them.

Searching or looting a body, container, or location implies taking found items unless the player explicitly states otherwise.

Completed transactions are TWO-WAY: if currency is removed for a purchase, the received goods MUST be added. If goods are handed over, payment MUST be added.

TRANSACTION TIMING: A transaction requires BOTH a stated price AND the player explicitly paying or agreeing AFTER the price is stated. "I'll take it" before a price is quoted is NOT confirmation. An NPC placing items on a counter is NOT a completed transaction. Only update when the player actually hands over currency or confirms the final price.

Quantities must match what actually changed. Do not add or remove an entire stack unless the story clearly shows the full stack changed hands or was consumed.

Perform the update immediately once actual change of hands occurs.

### Combat Item Rules

Consumables (potions, scrolls, arrows, thrown weapons) are removed when used in combat.

EQUIPMENT DURABILITY: Normal combat does NOT damage or destroy equipped weapons or armor. Hits, blocks, parries, clashes, and impacts are expected wear. Vivid combat narration -- sparks flying, dents forming, leather scoring -- is dramatic flavor, NOT mechanical damage. Do not interpret combat descriptions as equipment destruction.

Equipment damage ONLY occurs from EXTRAORDINARY events:
- A targeted attack explicitly intended to destroy the specific item
- Environmental destruction (acid, lava, disintegration magic)
- Powerful magic specifically described as shattering or unmaking gear
- The player deliberately sacrificing or abandoning an item
- Explicit narrative statement that the item is broken beyond use

If an extraordinary event occurs: remove the original and add a "Damaged [Item Name]" version, equipping it automatically. A "Damaged" item still functions and is only destroyed if ANOTHER extraordinary event specifically targets it. Normal combat does NOT destroy "Damaged" items.

 When uncertain, preserve the item.

### Tier Naming

When a player acquires a NEW equippable item (weapon, armor, jewelry, accessory) or alchemical items like potions or poisons ONLY, not already in their inventory or the world item list, ALWAYS prepend a rarity tier.

Tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
- Common: shop purchases, mundane locations, basic finds
- Uncommon: quest rewards, skilled crafts, superior quality, NPC gifts
- Rare: enchanted items, dungeon loot, magical properties, major quest rewards
- Epic: boss drops, powerful enchantments, named history
- Legendary: Lunarian relics

Format: "[Tier] [Item Name]" -- e.g. "Common Iron Dagger", "Rare Enchanted Rapier".
Flavor items have NO tier prefix -- e.g. "Wine", "Bread", "Iron Key", "Letter".

MATERIAL TIERS: Crafting materials also get tier prefixes.
- Common: ordinary, basic, widely available
- Uncommon: high-grade, superior, refined, premium
- Rare: magical, glowing, ancient, enchanted
- Epic: from extremely dangerous or unique sources
- Legendary: Lunarian materials, mythical substances

Do NOT tier-prefix:
- Items already in inventory or the world item list (reuse exact existing names)
- Non-equippable or flavor items like: documents, food, drinks, tools, keys, misc objects etc

### Crafting Accuracy

When crafting occurs, remove ONLY the specific materials the story describes being consumed. Match material names exactly from inventory. If the story uses iron ore, remove iron ore

### Category and Slot Reference

Armor: head, body, cloak, legs, feet, hands
Weapon: weapon
Consumable: consumable
Accessory: accessory
Necklace: necklace
Ring: ring
Miscellaneous: no slot
Currency: no slot

### Before Output, Confirm
1. Did items physically change possession? If not, output nothing.
2. Does any entry match the NEVER list? If yes, remove it.
3. For purchases: did the player confirm AFTER a price was stated?
4. Am I removing equipped gear? Was there an extraordinary destruction event? If just normal combat, preserve the item.
