HIGHEST PRIORITY INSTRUCTIONS
CONTENT REQUIREMENTS FOR hiddenInfo:
Determine: SENTIENT (intelligent, speaking creature) or NOT-SENTIENT (animal, monster, non-intelligent creature or construct).
hiddenInfo for sentient NPCs must be at least 5000 characters. Details must cross-reference between sections -- a Background relationship should visibly affect Personality behavior, a personality trait should shape Combat approach. When a detail appears in multiple sections it must do different work each time, not restate the same implication.
--- IF SENTIENT NPCs ---
Write hiddenInfo as three labeled fields. Content MUST be dense behavioral prose -- never list adjectives or traits.
Occupation is one fact about the NPC, not their identity. No more than half the sentences in any field should relate to their job.
Background: 10-12sentences minimum covering ALL of the following:
1. Name, age, race, occupation, and how they earn their living.
2. How they arrived at their current situation -- what is unresolved or still affecting them. Not a full biography or job origin story.
3. At least one local relationship. Describe the dynamic: "resents the guild clerk who denied her" not "knows a guild clerk."
4. What they currently want or care about. Can be mundane, satisfied, contradictory, or self-interested.
5. One or two opinions about the world around them -- faction, local authority, neighbors, a recent event. Make opinions specific and voiced, not abstract.
These 5 points are the minimum skeleton. After covering them, continue building with specific events, daily-life details, and concrete circumstances until the character feels like a person, not a completed checklist.
Personality:13-14 sentences minimum covering ALL of the following:
1. How they talk. Sentence length, vocabulary, verbal habits, favorite expressions. What makes them talkative versus terse.
2. Default social behavior AND what changes it -- always pair these. Do not write single static traits like "she is cautious."
3. Low-stakes behavior -- what they do when idle, what small things they enjoy, what they complain about.
4. Social calibration -- how they treat people above, below, or equal to them in status.
5. What they deflect or lie about, if anything. Many characters are simply open, blunt, or indifferent -- not everyone hides something.
6. One area of real competence or strong conviction beyond their occupation.
Every point should get roughly equal space. Do not let any single detail dominate.
Combat: 5-6 sentences covering ALL of the following:
1. Fighting style, aggression level, preferred range.
2. What makes them start, escalate, or abandon a fight. How they react when wounded.
3. One tactical habit or signature behavior that makes their combat feel distinct.
4. Combat-trained characters fight like it. Civilians panic, submit, lash out, or improvise.
Format: "hiddenInfo": "Background: ...\n\nPersonality: ...\n\nCombat: ..."
--- IF NOT-SENTIENT ---
Write hiddenInfo as two labeled fields. Describe what the creature does, not what it "is."
Behavior: 3-5 sentences. Habitat, pack/solo dynamics, sensory traits, threat escalation, signs of its presence.
Combat: 3-5 sentences. How it attacks, preferred range, signature offense, reactions when wounded or outnumbered, retreat triggers.
Format: "hiddenInfo": "Behavior: ...\n\nCombat: ..."
 
ANTI-PATTERNS -- NEVER DO THIS:
Trait lists disguised as prose ("He is brave, cunning, and loyal")
Static traits without context ("She is suspicious" -- of whom? when? write the situation)
Single static trait instead of a paired behavior ("He is friendly" -- write who he is friendly with and when he is not)
Personality that restates the NPC's role ("meticulous administrator, adherent to process") -- write behavior beyond the job
Occupation-themed abilities ("Bureaucratic Obstruction" for a clerk)
Every NPC hiding secret pain behind a cheerful mask -- some people are straightforward
Relationships that exist outside the current location and can never appear in scene
Generic reactions ("gets angry when threatened") -- only include responses specific to THIS character
Personality for beasts or combat philosophy for rabbits
Repeating information across fields
Abilities the character could not plausibly have
Every sentence starting with "He" or "She" -- vary structure
 
Abilities
ABILITY GENERATION RULES:
Civilian: 7  abilities. Combat-trained:  10 abilities. Beast/Monster: 5 (instinct-driven, no social).
Each ability is a broad category, not a single move. Mix of attack, utility, and social for sentient NPCs. At least one attack.
Format: (<TYPE>) <ABILITY NAME>: <ABILITY DESCRIPTION>
Example: (attack) Greatsword Mastery: Wields heavy two-handed blades with devastating sweeping strikes, overhead chops, and momentum to cleave through armor and multiple foes.
Adapt to each character. Do not copy the example.
